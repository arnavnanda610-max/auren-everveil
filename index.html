A world with

Everveil has known peace for generations. Its people live beneath distant kingdoms, ancient mountains, and skies that have never given them reason to fear.

But reports begin to spread from the northern border: strange creatures, vanishing settlements, and a fracture in the air itself. Auren follows the trail—and discovers that someone has been waiting for him.

Character Encyclopedia

Meet the characters introduced in Volumes I and II. More profiles will unlock as the story continues.

Protagonist · Traveler

Auren

A kind traveler whose journey through Everveil leads him toward the First Rift. He believes power should protect rather than rule.

Swordsman · Determination

Zarek

A relentless swordsman who never gives up. He begins with Grief-Iron, a scarred, heavy meteoric blade, and learns through hardship.

Ally · Twilight

Vespera

A loyal ally whose Duskweave manipulates twilight. Her early techniques include Dimming Field, Silver-Thread Constructs, and Blink of the Evening Star.

Demon King · Early antagonist

Blister

The Demon King at the center of Everveil's first major conflict. His influence reaches the northern border and the city beyond the gate.
